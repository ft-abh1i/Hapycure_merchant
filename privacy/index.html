1. Scope and who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data processed through the Hapycure Partner Portal and related partner-facing Hapycure services. It covers individuals who create or use Partner accounts and individuals whose information is submitted on behalf of a restaurant, home kitchen, mess, proprietorship, company or other food business.

It should be read together with the Hapycure Partner Terms of Service. This policy does not govern independent privacy practices of third-party websites or services that Hapycure does not control.

3. Where information comes from

We may receive information:

  • directly from you when you sign in, onboard, edit a profile, upload an image, create a listing or contact support;
  • from Google when you choose Google sign-in, according to the permissions and information shown during authentication;
  • from your device when you actively grant location permission;
  • from Hapycure admin/review workflows when a business or listing is approved, rejected or reviewed;
  • from customers, delivery partners, payment providers or support channels where necessary to resolve an order, safety, fraud or service issue; and
  • automatically from hosting, authentication, database, security and network infrastructure used to deliver the service.

4. How we use information

Hapycure may process Partner information to:

  • create, authenticate and secure accounts;
  • onboard and verify Partner businesses and maintain approval workflows;
  • publish approved business profiles, dishes, menus and mess plans;
  • enable location-based discovery, serviceability, delivery or operational features;
  • process or support orders, payments, settlements, cancellations and refunds where those features are enabled;
  • communicate service messages, policy updates, security alerts and support responses;
  • detect fraud, fake accounts, abuse, security threats and violations of Hapycure policies;
  • debug, maintain, improve and measure service reliability and user experience;
  • protect customers, Partners, Hapycure personnel and the public;
  • investigate complaints, food-safety concerns and disputes; and
  • comply with applicable law, regulatory directions, court orders and lawful government requests.

Hapycure will not use personal data for a new purpose that is incompatible with the purpose originally disclosed unless permitted by applicable law or appropriate notice/consent is provided.

5. Information visible to customers

Once a business or listing is approved and published, information intended for the marketplace may be visible to Hapycure customers. This can include the business name, business type, food type, business area/address or map/location information as needed for discovery/serviceability, operating status/hours, dishes, meal plans, menus, prices, dietary labels and uploaded images.

Do not place private personal information inside a public business name, description, menu or image. Hapycure may hide or remove information that appears to expose unnecessary personal data or create a safety risk.

6. How information is shared

We may share or make information available in the following situations:

  • Customers: approved marketplace/business information needed for discovery, ordering or service fulfilment.
  • Hapycure personnel and authorised reviewers: information needed for admin approval, support, moderation, safety, fraud and operations.
  • Delivery/service participants: limited information necessary to complete an order or service where such features are enabled.
  • Service providers: vendors that provide cloud hosting, databases, authentication, image storage, location/reverse-geocoding, communications, payments, security or technical support.
  • Business transfers: information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganisation or sale of relevant business/assets, subject to applicable law.
  • Legal and safety reasons: where reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce rights, prevent fraud, respond to lawful process or protect safety.

Hapycure does not sell Partner personal data for money. Where information is shared with processors/service providers, Hapycure expects them to process it for authorised purposes and subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Service providers and international processing

The Partner Portal currently relies on technology providers that may include Google/Firebase for authentication and database services, Cloudinary for uploaded image hosting/processing, Vercel for web hosting/deployment, and mapping/reverse-geocoding infrastructure for location features. Providers and architecture may change as the service evolves.

Depending on a provider's infrastructure, personal data may be processed or stored in India or other jurisdictions. Hapycure will handle cross-border processing in accordance with restrictions and requirements applicable under Indian law as they come into force.

8. Legal basis, consent and lawful processing

Hapycure processes digital personal data in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 as their relevant provisions come into force, as well as other applicable technology, consumer and sector-specific laws.

Depending on the activity, processing may be based on your consent, actions you voluntarily take to use requested services, performance/administration of the Partner relationship, compliance with law, or another basis permitted by applicable law.

Where Hapycure relies on consent, you may withdraw it through the available account controls or by contacting Hapycure. Withdrawal does not make prior lawful processing invalid and may prevent Hapycure from providing features that require the affected data.

9. Retention and deletion

Hapycure keeps personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, the active Partner relationship, security/fraud prevention, dispute handling, accounting, legal compliance and enforcement of rights.

When data is no longer needed, Hapycure may delete, anonymise or securely isolate it, subject to backups, technical limitations and legally required retention. Some records may be retained after account closure where needed for unresolved orders, payments, complaints, fraud/security investigations, tax/accounting obligations, regulatory requirements or legal claims.

You may request account/personal-data deletion using the privacy contact below. Hapycure will verify the request and act in accordance with applicable law. Removing an account may not automatically delete information that Hapycure is legally required or permitted to retain.

10. Security

Hapycure uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data, such as authenticated access, provider security controls, access restrictions and secure HTTPS transport where supported by the service. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

You also play a role in security: protect your Google/account credentials, use trusted devices, sign out from shared devices, and immediately report suspected unauthorised access.

11. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and verification of your request, you may have rights to:

  • obtain information about personal data being processed and relevant processing activities;
  • request correction, completion or updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • request erasure of personal data where retention is no longer required or otherwise permitted;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent;
  • raise a grievance about Hapycure's handling of personal data;
  • nominate another individual to exercise applicable rights in circumstances recognised by law; and
  • use any other right or remedy available under applicable law.

To protect your account and other people, Hapycure may ask for reasonable information to verify identity and authority before completing a request. Requests may be limited where law requires or permits continued processing or retention.

12. Children

The Hapycure Partner Portal is intended for adults who are at least 18 years old and authorised to operate or represent a food business. It is not intended for children. If Hapycure learns that a Partner account has unlawfully submitted a child's personal data, it may take steps to restrict or delete that information as appropriate.

13. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The Partner Portal and its authentication/infrastructure providers may use browser local storage, cookies or similar technologies to maintain sign-in state, remember Partner application data, support security, complete authentication and improve reliability. Some local application state may remain on a device until you log out, clear site data or the application removes it.

Blocking essential storage may prevent login or other core features from working correctly.

14. Data incidents

If Hapycure becomes aware of a personal-data breach or security incident, it will assess and respond to the incident and make notifications to affected individuals or authorities where required by applicable law. Partners must promptly report suspected compromise or accidental disclosure involving Hapycure data.

15. Changes to this policy

Hapycure may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in law, technology, service providers, features or data practices. The “Last updated” date will be revised, and material changes may also be communicated through the Partner Portal, email or another reasonable channel. Where applicable law requires consent for a change, Hapycure will seek it.
